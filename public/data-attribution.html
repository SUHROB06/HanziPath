HanziPath dictionary data sources

HanziPath combines separate, attributed data assets for HSK 2.0, HSK 3.0, examples and RU/UZ translations. Each asset keeps its own licence. This page records the sources and the changes made when preparing them for the app.

CC BY-SA 4.0

HSK 3.0 (2026) vocabulary and example sentences

Zhongdex by SayMei — zhongdex.org · source repository.

Required attribution: Zhongdex by SayMei — https://zhongdex.org — CC BY-SA 4.0. Contains data from CC-CEDICT (CC BY-SA 4.0) and the HSK 3.0 (2026) word list.

The HSK 3.0 word list, pinyin, POS information, English glosses and graded example sentences are derived from this dataset. HanziPath merged duplicate word/readings, retained source IDs and per-gloss attribution, normalized the data into HanziPath word keys, and attached the best available level-matched example to each reading.

Example sentences are source-curated but have not been individually reviewed by HanziPath. The source corpus reports 29,619 sentence records; 10,999 of 11,019 HSK 3.0 word/readings have an attached example (99.82%). The remaining 20 readings are left without an invented example.

CC-CEDICT attribution: This work uses data from CC-CEDICT, made available by MDBG under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence (CC BY-SA 4.0). Changes were made: entries were selected against the HSK 3.0 (2026) word list, merged with band metadata, re-keyed to HanziPath word-reading IDs, and reformatted.

Asset: /vocabulary-catalog-hsk30.json. Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0.

HSK 2.0 example sentences

Zhongdex by SayMei — zhongdex.org · source repository.

HanziPath matches graded Zhongdex example sentences to HSK 2.0 word/readings by simplified form and numbered pronunciation. 4,305 of 4,850 HSK 2.0 word/readings have an example in this build (88.76%). Source examples are marked as curated but not individually verified by HanziPath.

Asset: /vocabulary-examples-hsk20.json. Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0. It is kept separate from the HSK 2.0 CC BY 4.0 word-list asset to preserve each source licence.

CC BY 4.0

HSK 2.0 (2009) vocabulary and official glosses

Marpalo — HSK Vocabulary Dataset — source repository.

HanziPath uses the HSK 2.0 word-list fields and official glosses from this dataset. Duplicate POS rows for the same simplified word and reading are merged while the POS labels and level membership are retained. The separate full_definitions CC-CEDICT field is not included in this HSK 2.0 asset.

Asset: /vocabulary-catalog-hsk20.json. Licence: CC BY 4.0.

Russian and Uzbek translations

Maqsudjonpolatov — HSK Chinese–Uzbek–Russian vocabulary — Hugging Face dataset.

The dataset provides Russian and Uzbek glosses and is published under CC BY 4.0. The publisher describes the translations as AI-assisted and progressively reviewed. HanziPath keeps these translations marked as unverified; they should not be treated as a substitute for native-speaker review. The pinned CSV snapshot contributes 7,827 matched word/readings. An existing 11,147-entry HanziPath review bundle supplies 3,061 additional fallback matches. Together, available RU/UZ glosses cover 10,888 of 11,559 combined unique HSK word/readings (94.2%); 671 readings still need translation review. Missing glosses are not silently fabricated.

Fallback bundle attribution: public/vocabulary-review.json is published as CC BY-SA 4.0 and attributes HearMandarin (CC BY 4.0), Zitou (CC BY 4.0) and HanziBook / CC-CEDICT (CC BY-SA 4.0). Its source-unverified and AI-draft labels are preserved per translation field. The combined translation asset is therefore CC BY-SA 4.0.

Asset: /vocabulary-translations-ru-uz.json. Combined asset licence: CC BY-SA 4.0 (because it includes the CC BY-SA review fallback). The original Zitou CSV remains CC BY 4.0.

How the data is used

  • The HSK 3.0 corpus and its examples remain a separate CC BY-SA 4.0 asset. HSK 2.0 example sentences are also kept in a separate CC BY-SA 4.0 overlay.
  • The HSK 2.0 word list remains CC BY 4.0. RU/UZ translations and HSK 2.0 example sentences are separate CC BY-SA 4.0 overlays.
  • The app combines matching word/readings at runtime; it does not publish a single merged downloadable vocabulary database.
  • Membership, translation and example quality are tracked separately. RU/UZ glosses and source examples are not silently marked as human-verified.
  • These licences apply to the referenced data assets, not to the HanziPath application code.
